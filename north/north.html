Q-SUCCEED-NORTH

Quantum and Semiconductor Upskilling for Career Change through Experiential Education Deployment in Jefferson County

Empowering Adult Learners in Jefferson County

Q-SUCCEED-NORTH is an NSF-funded program that gives adult learners—including career changers, veterans, mid-career non-technical professionals, and people with no prior STEM background—structured exposure to semiconductor, optics, and quantum technology careers.

Based at Jefferson Community College, Q-SUCCEED-NORTH brings the Q-SUCCEED approach to Jefferson County. Participants take part in hands-on labs, workshops, and career exploration activities designed for people who have never worked in a technical field. No prior experience is needed.

By building foundational skills, confidence, and connections with employers, the program offers an accessible first step into fast-growing semiconductor and quantum-related industries and helps strengthen the local workforce.
